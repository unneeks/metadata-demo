# Glossary

## Organization
- **Employee**: An individual with an active or historical employment relationship.
- **Team**: A logical grouping of employees.

## Delivery
- **Work Item**: A unit of work tracked through a process.
- **Rework**: Work performed on an item after it has reached an agreed completion state.
- **Rework Rate**: The proportion of eligible work items classified as rework.

## Engineering
- **Change**: A managed deployment event.
- **Incident**: An unexpected disruption of service.
- **Change-Related Incident**: An incident associated with a change.

## People
- **Office Attendance Pattern**: An aggregated representation of workplace-location events. This is a contextual work-pattern signal and is not a direct measure of productivity.
